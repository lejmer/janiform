\section{Purpose}

Derive the conditional characteristic function and moment-generating function
of the compensated NIG trading-time block. This is the local transform that
later gets embedded in the affine schedule and terminal pricing derivations.

\section{Setup}

On one interval \(h\), condition on the decomposed return clocks
\[
\mathcal C_h
=
\sigma\!\left(B_{Z,h}^+,E_h^+,B_{Z,h}^-,E_h^-\right).
\]

The effective buy and sell return clocks are
\[
A_h^+
=
B_{Z,h}^+ + \eta_+E_h^+,
\qquad
A_h^-
=
B_{Z,h}^- + \eta_-E_h^-,
\]
with \(A_h^+\ge 0\), \(A_h^-\ge 0\), \(\eta_+>0\), and \(\eta_->0\).

Let \(L^+\) and \(L^-\) be independent NIG Levy drivers conditional on the
clock realization. Their characteristic exponents are
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{iuL_a^\pm}\right]
=
\exp\!\left(a\psi_\pm(u)\right),
\]
and their unit business-time means are
\[
m_\pm
=
\frac{d}{da}\mathbb E[L_a^\pm]\bigg|_{a=1}.
\]

The trading block is
\[
X_{\mathrm{tr},h}
=
\left(L_{A_h^+}^+ - A_h^+m_+\right)
-
\left(L_{A_h^-}^- - A_h^-m_-\right).
\]

\section{Result}

\noindent\textbf{Identity.} Conditional on \(\mathcal C_h\), the trading-block
characteristic function is
\[
\phi_{\mathrm{tr},h}(u\mid\mathcal C_h)
=
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{iuX_{\mathrm{tr},h}}\mid\mathcal C_h\right]
=
\exp\!\left(
A_h^+\left[\psi_+(u)-ium_+\right]
+
A_h^-\left[\psi_-(-u)+ium_-\right]
\right).
\]

For every \(q\) in the common moment domain, the conditional MGF is
\[
M_{\mathrm{tr},h}(q\mid\mathcal C_h)
=
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{qX_{\mathrm{tr},h}}\mid\mathcal C_h\right]
=
\exp\!\left(
A_h^+\left[K_+(q)-qm_+\right]
+
A_h^-\left[K_-(-q)+qm_-\right]
\right),
\]
where \(K_\pm(q)=\psi_\pm(-iq)\).

\section{Derivation}

Start from the compensated trading block:
\[
X_{\mathrm{tr},h}
=
L_{A_h^+}^+ - A_h^+m_+
-L_{A_h^-}^- + A_h^-m_-.
\]

For the CF, condition on \(\mathcal C_h\) and separate the deterministic
compensation terms:
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{iuX_{\mathrm{tr},h}}\mid\mathcal C_h\right]
=
e^{-iuA_h^+m_+}
e^{iuA_h^-m_-}
\mathbb E\!\left[
e^{iuL_{A_h^+}^+}
e^{-iuL_{A_h^-}^-}
\mid\mathcal C_h
\right].
\]

Conditional independence of the side drivers gives
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[
e^{iuL_{A_h^+}^+}
e^{-iuL_{A_h^-}^-}
\mid\mathcal C_h
\right]
=
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{iuL_{A_h^+}^+}\mid\mathcal C_h\right]
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{-iuL_{A_h^-}^-}\mid\mathcal C_h\right].
\]

Because \(L^\pm\) are Levy processes in business time and \(A_h^\pm\) are fixed
under \(\mathcal C_h\),
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{iuL_{A_h^+}^+}\mid\mathcal C_h\right]
=
\exp\!\left(A_h^+\psi_+(u)\right),
\qquad
\mathbb E\!\left[e^{-iuL_{A_h^-}^-}\mid\mathcal C_h\right]
=
\exp\!\left(A_h^-\psi_-(-u)\right).
\]

Multiplying the four exponential factors and collecting exponents yields
\[
\phi_{\mathrm{tr},h}(u\mid\mathcal C_h)
=
\exp\!\left(
A_h^+\left[\psi_+(u)-ium_+\right]
+
A_h^-\left[\psi_-(-u)+ium_-\right]
\right).
\]

The MGF derivation is the same calculation with \(q\) in place of \(iu\). The
buy-side raw log moment is \(A_h^+K_+(q)\). The sell-side raw log moment is
\(A_h^-K_-(-q)\), because the sell driver enters the return as
\(-L_{A_h^-}^-\). The compensation terms contribute \(-qA_h^+m_+\) and
\(+qA_h^-m_-\), giving
\[
M_{\mathrm{tr},h}(q\mid\mathcal C_h)
=
\exp\!\left(
A_h^+\left[K_+(q)-qm_+\right]
+
A_h^-\left[K_-(-q)+qm_-\right]
\right).
\]

The conditional first moment follows by differentiating the log-MGF at
\(q=0\). Since \(K_\pm'(0)=m_\pm\),
\[
\frac{d}{dq}\log M_{\mathrm{tr},h}(q\mid\mathcal C_h)\bigg|_{q=0}
=
A_h^+(m_+-m_+)+A_h^-(-m_-+m_-)
=
0.
\]

\section{Domain and validity}

The CF identity is valid for real \(u\) whenever the side NIG characteristic
exponents are valid at \(u\) and \(-u\). The MGF identity is valid for all
\(q\) such that both side log moments are finite:
\[
q\in\mathcal D_+,
\qquad
-q\in\mathcal D_-.
\]

For an NIG driver with parameters \((\alpha_\pm,\beta_\pm,\delta_\pm,\mu_\pm)\),
the real MGF domain is
\[
-\alpha_\pm-\beta_\pm
<
q
<
\alpha_\pm-\beta_\pm.
\]
Therefore the trading-block MGF requires \(q\) to satisfy the buy-side domain
and \(-q\) to satisfy the sell-side domain.

The clock variables must be finite and non-negative. This derivation conditions
on the clocks and does not yet average over Hawkes activity, long variance,
news, or gaps.

\section{Consequences}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The conditional trading-block transform is additive in the buy and sell return clocks.
    \item The sell-side contribution uses the negated transform argument because Janiform returns use the buy-minus-sell convention.
    \item Side mean compensation gives zero conditional trading drift for any realized non-negative clocks.
    \item If open-news and closed-gap increments are conditionally independent of the trading drivers after conditioning on their marks, the full interval CF multiplies this trading-block CF by the corresponding jump and gap transform factor.
\end{itemize}
