\section{Conditions}

The Hawkes and news transform domain requires every evaluated news mark MGF to
remain finite:
\[
    \operatorname{Re}\!\left(
        (\mathbf D(\tau)\cdot\mathbf r)a_c
        +qm_c
        +\frac{1}{2}q^2\sigma_c^2
    \right)
    <
    \frac{1}{\theta_U}
\]
for each news component \(c\) and \(\tau\in[0,\Delta]\).

The long-variance Riccati equation must have a finite solution \(V(\tau)\) on
the whole interval.
This is the usual CIR-style moment-domain condition induced by the quadratic
term in \(V\).

The pressure transform requires the finite matrix
\[
    \mathcal G^\Omega+\operatorname{diag}\left(
        (r_\Omega\gamma_\Omega-\rho_\Omega)e^\Omega b_1,
        \ldots,
        (r_\Omega\gamma_\Omega-\rho_\Omega)e^\Omega b_{K_\Omega}
    \right)
\]
and finite terminal pressure values.
For finite \(K_\Omega\), finite rates, finite support, and finite loadings, the
matrix exponential exists for every finite interval length.

\section{Pricing density}

The terminal density is admissible only when the denominator cumulant is finite
and the activity premia are strictly positive.
The martingale tilt is admissible only when both cumulants required by the
martingale equation are finite:
\[
    K_{t,T}(\theta,\log\pi_+,\log\pi_-,\rho_\Omega),
    \qquad
    K_{t,T}(\theta+1,\log\pi_+,\log\pi_-,\rho_\Omega).
\]

\section{Consequences}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Pressure admissibility is finite-state matrix-exponential admissibility.
    \item Pressure-domain failures are model-domain failures, not hidden fallback cases.
    \item The pressure risk price changes signed-imbalance exposure weighting under the terminal density without creating a second physical pressure model.
\end{itemize}
