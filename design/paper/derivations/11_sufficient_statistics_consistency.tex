\section{Purpose}

Show that seeded tick simulation, direct coarse generation,
sufficient-statistics sampling, and affine pricing preserve the same
interval decomposition.
The point is not that these paths use identical numerical algorithms, but that
they condition on and propagate the same mathematical objects.

\section{Setup}

On an interval \(k=[t_k,t_{k+1})\), define the decomposed sufficient-statistics
vector
\[
    \mathcal S_k
    =
    \left(
        B_{Z,k}^+,
        E_k^+,
        B_{Z,k}^-,
        E_k^-,
        N_k^+,
        N_k^-,
        \mathbf Y_{t_{k+1}},
        \Omega_{t_{k+1}},
        \Delta A_k^\Omega
    \right).
\]
Here \(B_{Z,k}^\pm\) are total long-variance-scaled baseline exposures,
\(E_k^\pm\) are total endogenous activity exposures, \(N_k^\pm\) are total
side event counts, \(\mathbf Y_{t_{k+1}}\) is the terminal Hawkes excitation
state, \(\Omega_{t_{k+1}}\) is the terminal market pressure state, and
\(\Delta A_k^\Omega\) is the market-pressure anchor-pressure movement.

Open-news and closed-gap return increments are additional additive return
components.
They are conditionally independent of the NIG trading-time draws after their
realized marks and routing state are fixed, and they affect future activity
through their Hawkes news excitation.

\section{Result}

\noindent\textbf{Proposition.} Conditional on \(\mathcal S_k\), realized news
and gap return components, and the interval boundary state, the interval
return law and the next structural boundary state are determined by the same
objects for seeded tick aggregation, direct coarse generation,
sufficient-statistics sampling, and affine pricing.

The trading-time return law is
\[
    X_{\mathrm{tr},h}
    =
    \left(L_{A_k^+}^+-A_k^+m_+\right)
    -
    \left(L_{A_k^-}^--A_k^-m_-\right),
    \qquad
    A_k^\pm=B_{Z,k}^\pm+\eta_\pm E_k^\pm.
\]
The full interval core return is
\[
    X_k
    =
    X_{\mathrm{tr},h}
    +\Delta M_k+\Delta G_k.
\]
The observed interval movement also includes the market-pressure anchor
movement and any residual-component relaxation:
\[
    \Delta X_k
    =
    \Delta A_k^\Omega
    +(\phi_k-1)D_k
    +X_k.
\]
The Hawkes boundary state is persisted explicitly:
\[
    \mathbf Y_{t_{k+1}}
    =
    e^{-\beta\Delta_k}\mathbf Y_{t_k}
    +C^{Y,+}_k
    +C^{Y,-}_k
    +C^{Y,J}_k,
\]
where \(C^{Y,+}_k\), \(C^{Y,-}_k\), and \(C^{Y,J}_k\) are the
event-time weighted terminal contributions from buy events, sell events, and
news activity loadings.
The market pressure boundary state and anchor movement are persisted
explicitly:
\[
    \Omega_{t_{k+1}}
    =
    \Psi_\Delta(
        \Omega_{t_k};
        N_k^+,
        N_k^-,
        \{\tau_m^\pm\},
        \Delta_k,
        \mu_{\Omega,k}^{\mathrm{sgn}}
    ),
\]
where \(\{\tau_m^\pm\}\) denotes the hidden or observed event placements needed
to compute the terminal pressure state and integrated pressure state.

\section{Derivation}

Start from the Janiform interval return decomposition:
\[
    X_k
    =
    \left(L_{A_k^+}^+-A_k^+m_+\right)
    -
    \left(L_{A_k^-}^--A_k^-m_-\right)
    +\Delta M_k+\Delta G_k.
\]
Given \(\mathcal S_k\), the side return clocks are fixed:
\[
    A_k^+=B_{Z,k}^++\eta_+E_k^+,
    \qquad
    A_k^-=B_{Z,k}^-+\eta_-E_k^-.
\]
By the conditional CF/MGF derivation, the NIG trading block depends on the
interval path only through \(A_k^+\) and \(A_k^-\).
Thus, any construction that preserves \(B_{Z,k}^\pm\) and \(E_k^\pm\)
preserves the conditional trading-time law.

The jump and gap components enter additively:
\[
X_k=X_{\mathrm{tr},h}+\Delta M_k+\Delta G_k.
\]
Open-window news returns are routed through \(\Delta M_k\), while closed-window
returns are stored as \(\Delta G_k\) until reopening.
Once these components are fixed, the total return is fixed up to the same
trading-time NIG draw.

For the Hawkes state, the continuous part over the interval is deterministic
decay between jumps.
Side events and news loadings add fixed excitation increments at their event
times.
Therefore, conditional on \(\mathbf Y_{t_k}\), \(\Delta_k\), and the
event-time weighted terminal contributions, the terminal state is
\[
    \mathbf Y_{t_{k+1}}
    =
    e^{-\beta\Delta_k}\mathbf Y_{t_k}
    +\sum_{m=1}^{N_k^+}e^{-\beta(t_{k+1}-\tau_m^+)}\mathbf k_+
    +\sum_{m=1}^{N_k^-}e^{-\beta(t_{k+1}-\tau_m^-)}\mathbf k_-
    +\sum_j e^{-\beta(t_{k+1}-\tau_j^J)}U_j^J\boldsymbol\rho.
\]
Seeded tick aggregation obtains these quantities by summing over generated
events.
Direct coarse and sufficient-statistics paths do not need to expose event tapes,
but they must materialize the same terminal contribution statistics internally
or persist the terminal state explicitly.

For market pressure, \(\Omega\) is a finite-state CTMC over side-balance
levels \(b_k\in[-1,1]\).
Let \(\mathbf p_k\) be the boundary probability vector and \(A^\Omega\) the
physical pressure generator.
Preserving the terminal pressure state and the integrated signed-balance
exposure
\[
    B_k^\Omega
    =
    \int_{t_k}^{t_{k+1}} b_{\Omega_u}\,dE_u^\Omega
\]
preserves anchor-pressure movement without requiring an event tape.
The conditional mean formulas are
\[
    \mathbf p_{k+1}
    =
    \exp(A^\Omega\Delta_k)\mathbf p_k
\]
and
\[
    \mathbb E[B_k^\Omega\mid \mathbf p_k]
    =
    \left(
        \int_0^{\Delta_k}\mathbf b^\top\exp(A^\Omega u)\mathbf p_k\,du
    \right)e_k^\Omega.
\]
The anchor increment is
\(\Delta A_k^\Omega=\gamma_\Omega B_k^\Omega\), and coarse workflows carry
the terminal pressure state and anchor increment rather than an event tape.

Affine pricing uses the same decomposition in transform form.
The affine schedule transform takes separate loadings for
\[
    B_Z^+,\quad E^+,\quad B_Z^-,
    \quad E^-,\quad N^+,\quad N^-,
    \quad Z_T,\quad \Omega_T,\quad \Delta A^\Omega,
\]
where the activity loadings apply to total activity exposure and the count
loadings apply to total events.
It evaluates the joint transform by propagating \((C,\mathbf D,V,W)\).
The terminal cumulant adapter in \texttt{07\_terminal\_cumulant\_adapter.tex}
maps pricing arguments into those same activity, count, and market-pressure
loadings.

Therefore, the four paths are consistent at the level of the transition law
whenever they preserve \(\mathcal S_k\), the routed news/gap return
components, and the shared boundary-state transition.

\section{Domain and validity}

The consistency statement is valid for admissible model parameters and
intervals satisfying the affine and simulation-domain constraints:
non-negative finite activity clocks, non-negative integer counts, positive
long-variance boundary and terminal states, finite market pressure
states, finite market-pressure anchor movement, finite routed news and
gap returns, valid Hawkes and market-pressure state updates, and finite
NIG side-driver transforms or samples.

The statistic \(\mathcal S_k\) is sufficient for the trading-time return law
and the structural transition pieces listed above.
It is not a claim that all diagnostic paths use identical random-number streams
or identical numerical approximations.
Distributional equality between seeded aggregation and direct coarse generation
must be checked by the paper's aggregation and sufficient-statistics plots.

\section{Consequences}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Tick aggregation and direct coarse generation can be compared on decomposed statistics, not only on final returns.
    \item Pricing uses the same baseline/endogenous activity split as simulation and sufficient-statistics sampling.
    \item Return-generation diagnostics should preserve trading-time, open-jump, closed-gap, market-pressure anchor, and total-return components.
    \item Boundary-state updates should be tested against the same Hawkes and market-pressure transition maps used by event simulation.
\end{itemize}
